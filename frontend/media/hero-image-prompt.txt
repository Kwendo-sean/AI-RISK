PROMPT FOR GEMINI (hero image, square 1:1, saved as frontend/media/hero.png)

A cinematic square photograph, 1:1 aspect ratio, of a single focused professional
at work in a dark studio-lit workspace — a young African engineer in a plain
work shirt, seated three-quarter view at a wide desk, hands resting on a
keyboard, looking at a large monitor. On the screen, a clean geometric interface
glows: simple charts and neat rows of data, softly out of focus. Beside the
monitor sit real tools of the trade — a notebook, a pen, a coffee cup, a small
hardware component — so the frame reads as human craft and machine assistance
sharing one desk.

Lighting: low-key and controlled. Deep near-black background (#080808), the
subject lit from the left by soft warm key light, with a restrained red rim
light (#ff443d) tracing the shoulder and the edge of the monitor. One thin band
of red glow from the screen falls across the desk surface.

Composition: calm, ordered and symmetrical. The subject sits left of centre with
clear negative space to the right. Straight, deliberate lines — desk edge,
monitor frame, wall seam. Nothing floating, nothing shattered, no scattered
particles, no exploding fragments, no collage.

Style: editorial documentary photography, 50mm lens, shallow depth of field,
natural skin tones, fine film grain, high contrast, muted palette of black,
charcoal, warm skin tone and a single red accent.

NEGATIVE / AVOID: no glowing robot, no humanoid android, no brain or circuit-board
metaphor, no floating holograms, no binary code or matrix rain, no cyberpunk
neon clutter, no abstract fragmented geometry, no double exposure, no text,
no logos, no watermark, no distorted hands or extra fingers.
